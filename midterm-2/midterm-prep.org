Above is the midterm we gave last year for this course.  You'll notice
that many of the problems have already appeared as homework
assignments; the workbook consists of every problem we've ever asked
in this course.  The primary value of the exams is to give you a sense
of how the exam will be structured, and what you should expect both in
terms of difficulty and length.

As far as topics, you should be familiar with everything up to and
include the lecture on Dimension and Rank (October 29), excluding the
material on Computer Graphics (October 22).  This includes everything
from the [[https://nmmull.github.io/cs132-f26/midterm/midterm-prep.html][Midterm 1 prep material]].  There will generally be a greater
emphasis on material after what appeared on Midterm 1.
